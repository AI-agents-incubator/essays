# Risk Policy

Purpose: define how operational risks are identified and mitigated.

Owner: `review-and-integration-agent`

Risk categories:
- Scope drift
- Missing artifacts
- State layer inconsistency
- Untracked handoffs
- Benchmark non-compliance

Risk handling:
- Log risks in `knowledge/decision_log.md`.
- Add mitigations to `execution/status_board.md`.
- If risk blocks progress, escalate per `policies/escalation_policy.md`.

Update rules:
- Proposed by `review-and-integration-agent`, approved by `engineering-manager`.
