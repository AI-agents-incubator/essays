# Skill: brief-to-spec

Purpose:
- Convert a product brief into an engineering spec.

When to use:
- After a new product brief is created.

Required inputs:
- `product/active_product_briefs/`
- `engineering/engineering_spec_template.md`

Expected outputs:
- New engineering spec in `engineering/`.
- Updated `engineering/task_graph.md`.

Artifacts updated:
- `engineering/ES-*.md`
- `engineering/task_graph.md`
